# Glossary & Index
## Glossary

:::{glossary}
: Agent of change
 An individual, group, or organisation that contributes to moving a situation from its current state towards a desired future through its ability to influence actions.

Sustainable transitions
: Processes of changing unsustainable systems and practices
  towards more sustainable ways of living.
  See also {term}`Societal transformation`.

Societal transformation
: Fundamental changes in social structures, institutions,
  behaviours, and relationships.
  Related concept: {term}`Sustainable transitions`.

:::
