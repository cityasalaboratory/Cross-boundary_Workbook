2. Connect with and define the challenge

Decide which real-life example of a challenge you want to explore further through the practical exercises.
By Juliette Cortes-Arevalo, Fatima-Zahra Abou Eddahab-Burke & Thorsten Philipp
Updated September 2026
Examples of interconnected societal and environmental systems.

Module summary¶

Cities are key centres of human activity, creativity, and knowledge and major contributors to interconnected social, economic, and environmental challenges. At the same time, they bring diverse people together, making them places to collaborate and explore alternative ways of thinking and acting. Do you perceive your city, village or community or the spot where you work, learn and live as a space to change everyday practices, live as yourself with others, and develop sustainable ways of living? This module elaborates on the City as a Laboratory and guides you through seven tasks to help revisiting and sharing how did you connect and define with a real-life example of a challenge that as a basis for developing and refining your learning journey as illustrated through an individual portfolio, parts of which you will share with others.

Learning Objectives¶

  • Explain the “City as a Laboratory” idea as a shared space for experimentation and developing knowledge.

  • Define the boundaries of the chosen real-life example.

  • Identify the role of cross-boundary collaborations in supporting societal transitions.

  • Describe the main steps and strategy for change, transformation and learning.

  • Reflect on personal motivations, roles, and capabilities for acting as an agent of change.
