Glossary

This glossary is being developed and will gradually include key Workbook terms as they appear.

Agent of change
An individual, group, or organisation capable of exercising agency to influence the direction of change. Agency refers to the ability of an actor to become relevant within a particular context and to shape actions. Source: Simoens et al., 2022, following Leipold & Winkel, 2017's definition (p. 524). First introduced in: See Paragraph
Service design
A service is the way in which an organisation, through people, processes, technologies, and environments, enables someone to achieve a desired outcome, thereby creating value through that interaction. Source: To add. First introduced in: See 4.1 Beyond human-centred design
Sustainability or survivability
Sustainability focuses on long-term well-being, whereas survivability focuses on the capacity to endure and meet immediate needs under challenging conditions. Both are shaped by interconnected social, economic, and environmental factors. Source: Halawa, 2024. First introduced in: See Paragraph
Sustainable transitions
Processes of changing unsustainable systems and practices towards more sustainable ways of living. See also Societal transformation.
Societal transformation
Fundamental changes in social structures, institutions, behaviours, and relationships. Related concept: Sustainable transitions.
