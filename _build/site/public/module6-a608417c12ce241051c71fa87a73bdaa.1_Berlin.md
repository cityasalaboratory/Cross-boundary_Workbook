<div class="section-subtitle">
A day at the first City as a Laboratory Knowledge Exchange Masterclass in a Berlin innovation hub, connecting people across sectors to drive change.
</div>
<div class="author-block">
By Tim Noppeney, communications and strategist consultant
</div>

<div class="event-callout">
<p><strong>The Knowledge Exchange Masterclass</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>What:</strong> A one-day training course in transdisciplinary methods for professionals from business, civil society, public administration, and cultural organisations.</li>
<li><strong>Part of:</strong> CAL:TS – The City as a Laboratory, an Erasmus+ co-funded collaboration of TU Berlin, TU Delft, and Politecnico di Milano within the ENHANCE alliance.</li>
<li><strong>2026 edition:</strong> Held on 17 September at MotionLab Berlin, with 12 participants. Facilitated by Thorsten Philipp, Nina Schmulius, and Jan Kratzer, with support from City as A Laboratory colleagues Juliette Cortes-Arevalo and Valentina Facoetti.</li>
<li><strong>Methods:</strong> User-driven innovation and storytelling were introduced through dedicated activities. Citizen science, real-world and living labs, and service learning were explored through open discussions and a speed-dating format.</li>
<li><strong>Participant feedback:</strong> All 10 respondents said the training deepened their understanding of transdisciplinary approaches and that they gained a positive network.</li>
<li><strong>Next edition:</strong> Delft, 2 October 2027, with collaborators from the <a href="https://vpdelta.tudelftcampus.nl/">VPDelta innovation program</a>.</li>
</ul>
</div>
