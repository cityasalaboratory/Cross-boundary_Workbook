## Module summary
This Cross-boundary Workbook of the City as A Laboratory project is the result of a joint effort by TU Berlin, Politecnico di Milano and TU Delft within the ENHANCE Alliance of European Universities of Technology. It is an open-access resource that supports reflective practice by encouraging learners to critically examine their experiences, intended actions, and assumptions, strengthening transdisciplinary learning and the co-creation of knowledge from, for, and with society. As such, the Workbook encourages citizens including students, educators, practitioners, policymakers and community members to collectively address interconnected social, economic, and environmental challenges.

The Workbook consists of learning modules, each with their reference list, dedicated tasks with worksheets for hands-on practice to compile into a Learning Journey Portfolio and a glossary of terms covering all modules. Each module combines theoretical insights with audiovisual explainers accompanied by cases and tasks from various sources. Cases present real-life examples of collaboration, while the tasks offer transferable learning activities that readers can apply in their own context. Together, the modules create opportunities to connect, explore ideas, reflect and exchange experiences through events for transformation and learning that readers can join or organise by themselves.

By integrating diverse perspectives and knowledge types, this Workbook strengthens the learner's ability to navigate complexity and supports the co-creation of sustainable practices across disciplines, sectors and cultural contexts. 

## Learning Objectives

- <span style="color:#0089A6; font-weight:700;">Explain</span> the motivation, structure, expected use, contributions to and benefits from this Workbook.

- <span style="color:#0089A6; font-weight:700;">Define</span> key concepts such as change, transition, transformation and sustainability mean to you.

- <span style="color:#0089A6; font-weight:700;">Develop</span>  the willingness to apply the Workbook content to your personal development by starting to compile a Learning Journey Portfolio.
